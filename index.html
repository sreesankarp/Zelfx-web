For Shop Owners

Run your shop on autopilot

Set it up once. Zelfx handles pricing, security, and reporting.

You set the prices

You can customize the pricing — like charge ₹5 for B&W and ₹20 for Color. Add premiums for glossy paper. The app automatically reads the customer's document and charges exactly what it's worth.

Works with your printers

If it connects to your Windows or Mac computer, Zelfx can use it. Inkjet, Laser, A4, or A3—we support the hardware you already own.

Zero Data Retention (ZDR)

Our Zero Data Retention (ZDR) policy processes every file in real time and deletes it immediately after printing — nothing is ever stored. Say goodbye to cluttered shop computers filled with strangers' personal IDs.

Track every penny

Our Desktop App acts as your command center. View a beautiful daily dashboard of every print job, total revenue, and export reports for your accounting.
